# pierrecinema/ai-music-studio

Free, open-source AI music studio and DAW for Windows. Make songs, seamless loops and stems with AI on your own PC (ACE-Step, MusicGen, Demucs), then arrange them in a full studio with mixer, piano roll and automation. Runs on CPU, NVIDIA, AMD and Intel. No account, no subscription.
