# tradoor-terminal/tradoor

Open-source crypto trading terminal in the browser: charts, DOM, footprint, TPO market profile, liquidations and order flow from 15 exchanges, your own indicators in Frost Script, a journal and paper trading. Rust, WebAssembly, WebGL2.
