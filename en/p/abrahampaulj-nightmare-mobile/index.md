# AbrahamPaulJ/nightmare-mobile

On-device ComfyUI style node graph for Stable Diffusion on Android. Local SD1.5, SDXL, Flux, Z-image, Anima, Qwen Image 2.1, Krea 2, text to image, img2img, inpainting, outpainting, controlnet, LoRA, IPAdapter and WAN 2.2 5B video generation on the Qualcomm Hexagon NPU. Fully offline, no server, no account.
