# oguzeldereli/ttf-ternary

Two-timescale threshold flips (TTF): training and converting ternary {-1,0,1} LLMs without full-precision master weights. ~1 byte of optimizer state per weight; 23B trains on a 16 GB GPU, Qwen3.8-27B converted and recovered on one 24 GB card.
