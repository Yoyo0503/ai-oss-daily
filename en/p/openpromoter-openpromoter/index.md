# openpromoter/openpromoter

Open-source affiliate and referral program software for SaaS. A self-hosted alternative to Rewardful, FirstPromoter and Tolt with Stripe, Paddle and Lemon Squeezy tracking, fraud checks, partner payouts and AI. 0% payout fees.
