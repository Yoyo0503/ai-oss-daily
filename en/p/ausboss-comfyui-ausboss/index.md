# ausboss/ComfyUI-AusBoss

ComfyUI nodes I made for my own workflows: click or type to mask a thing, crop, rotate and pad by dragging handles, inpaint and outpaint that keep your original pixels, tiled upscale, a video trim timeline, folder batches, layout boxes and a LoRA stack. I keep adding more.
