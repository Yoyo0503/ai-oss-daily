# rsalianto/tradoor

Open-source crypto trading terminal in the browser: TradingView-style charts, TPO market profile, footprint, liquidations and order flow from Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget and Hyperliquid, with a trading journal and paper trading. Built with Rust, WebAssembly and WebGL2.
